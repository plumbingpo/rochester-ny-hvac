Keeping Your Buffalo HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
